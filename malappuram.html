MALAPPURAM

Malappuram, located in northern Kerala, is known for its rich cultural blend shaped by history, religion, and trade. The district has a strong influence of the Mappila Muslim community, reflected in its traditions, festivals, cuisine, and art forms like Mappila songs and Oppana dance. Alongside this, Hindu and Christian communities contribute to a diverse and harmonious social fabric. Malappuram is also famous for its deep-rooted passion for football, which plays a big role in local identity and youth culture. Geographically, Malappuram features a mix of rolling hills, rivers, and coastal plains, with the Bharathapuzha River being a major natural landmark. The environment is lush and green, with coconut groves, paddy fields, and small hill ranges creating scenic landscapes. The climate is tropical, with heavy monsoon rains supporting agriculture and biodiversity. The people are predominantly of Dravidian origin, with a long history of cultural exchange due to Arab trade links, making the district unique in both heritage and lifestyle.
