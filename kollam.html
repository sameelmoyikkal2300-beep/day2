KOLLAM

Kollam, located in southern Kerala, has a rich cultural heritage shaped by its history as a major port and trading center. It was one of the earliest regions in Kerala to establish trade links with foreign countries, which influenced its traditions, cuisine, and lifestyle. The district is home to diverse communities—Hindus, Muslims, and Christians— living together harmoniously, and it is known for festivals, temple arts, and coir-making industries that form an important part of local culture. Geographically, Kollam is famous for its extensive backwaters, especially the scenic Ashtamudi Lake, which is one of the largest lakes in Kerala. The district features a mix of coastal areas, wetlands, rivers, and low hills, creating a rich and diverse environment. The tropical climate with heavy monsoon rainfall supports agriculture, fishing, and biodiversity. Its natural beauty and waterways make Kollam an important destination for tourism and economic activity.
