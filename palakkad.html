PALAKKAD

Palakkad, located in central Kerala, is known for its rich cultural heritage influenced by both Kerala and neighboring Tamil Nadu. This blend is reflected in its language, cuisine, festivals, and traditions. The district has a harmonious mix of Hindu, Muslim, and Christian communities, with temples and classical art forms like Kathakali and Carnatic music playing an important role in its cultural life. Agriculture is central to the lifestyle here, and the region is often called the “granary of Kerala” due to its vast paddy fields. Geographically, Palakkad is famous for the Palakkad Gap, a natural pass in the Western Ghats that connects Kerala with Tamil Nadu. The district features wide plains, rivers, and lush green fields, making it distinct from the hillier regions of Kerala. The tropical climate, with strong monsoon winds passing through the gap, supports farming and biodiversity. Its combination of fertile land, unique geography, and cultural diversity makes Palakkad an important region in Kerala.
