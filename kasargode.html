KASARGOD

Kasargod, the northernmost district of Kerala, is known for its rich cultural diversity and linguistic variety. The region is home to multiple communities speaking Malayalam, Tulu, Kannada, and Konkani, reflecting a unique blend of traditions and lifestyles. Folk art forms like Theyyam are widely practiced, and festivals of different religions are celebrated together, showing strong communal harmony. The people are mainly of Dravidian origin, shaped by centuries of cultural exchange due to its location near Karnataka. Geographically, Kasaragod features a mix of coastal plains, rivers, and low hills, with the Arabian Sea forming its western boundary. The district is famous for the historic Bekal Fort, which stands along the coastline and symbolizes its historical importance. The environment is lush and green, with coconut groves, paddy fields, and backwaters supported by a tropical monsoon climate. Its scenic landscapes and cultural richness make Kasaragod a unique gateway to Kerala.
