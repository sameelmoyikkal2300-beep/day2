KOZHIKODE

Kozhikode, located on the Malabar Coast of Kerala, has a rich cultural heritage shaped by centuries of trade and history. It was an important port where traders from Arabia, China, and Europe arrived, making it a center of cultural exchange. The district is famously associated with Vasco da Gama’s arrival in India, which marked a major historical event. Kozhikode is known for its warm hospitality, diverse communities, traditional Malabar cuisine, and contributions to literature and education. Geographically, Kozhikode features a mix of coastal plains, rivers, and small hill ranges extending towards the Western Ghats. The district is famous for its beautiful beaches, especially Kozhikode Beach, which is a popular spot for locals and tourists. The tropical climate, with heavy monsoon rains, supports lush greenery and agriculture. Its combination of natural beauty, historical importance, and cultural richness makes Kozhikode a significant region in Kerala.
