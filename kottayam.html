KOTTAYAM

Kottayam, located in central Kerala, is known for its strong cultural and educational background. Often called the “land of letters, latex, and lakes,” Kottayam has a high literacy rate and a rich tradition of publishing and literature. The district has a diverse population of Hindus, Christians, and Muslims living in harmony, with Christian communities playing a significant role in its social and cultural development. Traditional festivals, religious practices, and rubber cultivation are important aspects of life here. Geographically, Kottayam features a unique landscape of midlands, hills, and backwater regions. It is closely associated with the scenic Kumarakom area, known for its backwaters, bird sanctuary, and tourism. The district has fertile land covered with rubber plantations, paddy fields, and coconut trees, supported by a tropical monsoon climate. Rivers, lakes, and lush greenery contribute to its natural beauty, making Kottayam a blend of nature, education, and cultural richness.
