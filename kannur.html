KANNUR

Kannur, situated in northern Kerala, is known for its vibrant cultural heritage and strong traditional roots. The district is especially famous for the ritual art form Theyyam, which reflects deep spiritual beliefs and local folklore. Kannur has a rich history of political movements and resistance, earning it a reputation as a land of strong social awareness. The people belong mainly to Dravidian ethnic roots, with Hindu, Muslim, and Christian communities contributing to a diverse and harmonious culture, along with traditional crafts like handloom weaving. Geographically, Kannur features a beautiful combination of coastal plains, rivers, and small hill ranges. The district lies along the Arabian Sea, giving it scenic beaches, backwaters, and fishing villages that support the local economy. The tropical climate, with heavy monsoon rains, maintains lush greenery and fertile land. Natural beauty, along with cultural richness, makes Kannur a unique blend of tradition, environment, and history in Kerala.
