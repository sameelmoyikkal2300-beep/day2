WAYANAD

Wayanad, located in the Western Ghats of Kerala, is known for its rich tribal heritage and serene cultural landscape. The district is home to several indigenous communities whose traditions, customs, and lifestyles remain closely connected to nature. Alongside tribal culture, people from different religious backgrounds live together, creating a harmonious social environment. Agriculture, especially the cultivation of spices like pepper, cardamom, and coffee, plays a major role in shaping the culture and economy of Wayanad. Geographically, Wayanad is one of the most scenic and forest-rich districts in Kerala, featuring misty hills, dense forests, waterfalls, and wildlife sanctuaries. It lies at a higher altitude compared to most districts in the state, giving it a cooler climate. The region is famous for places like Edakkal Caves, which contain ancient carvings. The tropical monsoon climate supports rich biodiversity, making Wayanad a key destination for eco-tourism and nature lovers.
